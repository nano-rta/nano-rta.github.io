What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, and data from attacks and theft. Every time you go online, log in to school, or send a message, your information travels through systems that need protection.

It matters because so much of life is online now: school work, bank accounts, photos, and jobs. If someone breaks in, they can steal private information, lock your files, or pretend to be you.

Three Big Ideas

People who work in cybersecurity often talk about three goals:

  • Keep it private: only the right people can see your information.
  • Keep it correct: nobody changes your information without permission.
  • Keep it working: you can reach your information when you need it.

Tip: use a different password for each account.

Most attacks work because someone clicked something they should not have.
