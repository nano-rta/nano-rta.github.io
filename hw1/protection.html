How to Protect Yourself

You do not need to be an expert to stay safer online. These six habits help a lot:

  1. Use a strong password that is long and different for every account.
  2. Turn on two-factor authentication when a site offers it.
  3. Update your phone and computer when updates appear.
  4. Back up your important files, so you can get them back if something goes wrong.
  5. Be careful on public Wi-Fi, and do not log in to important accounts on it.
  6. Think before you click a link or open an attachment, even if the message looks real.

Turn on two-factor authentication everywhere you can. It is one of the easiest ways to stay safe.

What is two-factor authentication?

It is a second check after your password. After you type your password, the site asks for a code from your phone. Even if someone steals your password, they still cannot get in without that code.
